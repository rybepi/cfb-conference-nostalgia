CFB Conference Nostalgia

The 2026 season, played out in the 2004 conferences. Records count only games between teams that shared a conference in 2004. Top two from each league make the playoff.

Power Tier 1

ACC, Big East, Big Ten, Big 12, Pac-10, SEC. Twelve teams; seeds 1 to 4 get byes.

Power Tier 2

C-USA, MAC, Mountain West, Sun Belt, WAC. Ten teams; seeds 1 to 6 get byes.

Conference standings

Ranked by 2004-conference record, then head-to-head among tied teams, current-conference record, overall record, and point differential in 2004-conference games. Dashed line marks the playoff cut.

† Program was not in the FBS in 2004 and has been placed in the nearest 2004 conference.

Results: ESPN public scoreboard feed, refreshed Sundays at 6 a.m. Eastern. Overall and current-conference records count games against FBS teams only. Teams with no games against 2004 conference mates show 0-0 and fall through to the next tiebreaker.
